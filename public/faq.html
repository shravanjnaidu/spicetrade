Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about buying and selling on BigSpice B2B Marketplace.

🌐 General

BigSpice is a B2B online marketplace where businesses can buy and sell products and services across all categories — from spices, food commodities, and agricultural produce to industrial goods and professional services. Founded in 2022 by Spice Cloud Technologies (Canada), it connects verified sellers with wholesale buyers across the globe, making business sourcing simple, transparent, and efficient.
Creating an account, browsing listings, posting requirements, and messaging are all free. Sellers can list their products and services at no cost. Optional paid advertising placements (banner ads) are available for businesses that want premium visibility on the homepage.
BigSpice is open to all B2B product and service categories. Common categories include:
  • Spices & Herbs, Tea & Coffee, Oils & Fats
  • Pulses, Grains & Cereals, Sugar & Sweeteners
  • Dairy, Fruits & Vegetables, Seafood
  • Industrial goods, Raw materials, Packaging
  • Professional services and B2B services
Sellers tag their listings with products or services, and buyers can post requirements using matching tags so the right suppliers are notified automatically.
BigSpice is a global B2B platform headquartered in Canada. Businesses from any country can join as buyers or sellers. Sellers can specify their shipping or service locations, and buyers can search for local or international suppliers. Both domestic and cross-border trade are fully supported.
Yes. Passwords are stored using industry-standard hashing (never in plain text). All data is stored securely in our database. We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties. Sensitive fields such as tax numbers and addresses are only used for account verification and communication purposes.

🛒 For Buyers

Click Sign up and choose Become a Buyer. Fill in your business name, email, and a password — it takes less than a minute. Once signed in, you can browse all listings, message sellers, save items to your wishlist, and post buying requirements.
No. Anyone can browse product and service listings and store pages without an account. However, you need a free account to message sellers, save items to your wishlist, leave reviews, or post buying requirements.
Log in and go to your Dashboard. Click Post a Requirement and fill in the title, description, and the product or service tag that best matches what you need, along with quantity and deadline. BigSpice then automatically forwards your requirement to all sellers who have listings with that matching tag, so relevant suppliers can reach out to you directly.
When you post a requirement with a product or service tag, BigSpice automatically notifies all sellers whose listings share that tag. They receive an email with your requirement details and a link to respond, so you get targeted responses from genuinely relevant suppliers without having to search for them yourself.
Open any product or service listing and click the Message Seller button. You’ll be taken to the messaging section where you can start a direct conversation. All messages are private between you and the seller. You must be signed in to send messages.
Yes. Click the wishlist (heart) icon on any product card or listing page to save it for later. Access all your saved items from the Wishlist page in your account.
Navigate to the seller’s store page or the listing you dealt with. Click Leave a Review, give a star rating, and write your feedback. Reviews help the community make informed sourcing decisions and reward reliable suppliers.
BigSpice is a marketplace and matchmaking platform. Payments, shipping, and delivery arrangements are negotiated directly between buyer and seller via the built-in messaging system. We recommend confirming payment terms, shipping timelines, minimum order quantities, and product quality standards before finalising any transaction.

🏪 For Sellers

Click Sign up as a Seller. You’ll provide your name, business email, password, store name, business type, the products or services you offer, and your location. Once your account is created, you can immediately start adding listings from your seller dashboard.
BigSpice is open to all B2B products and services. You can list physical goods (agricultural produce, industrial materials, food commodities, packaged products), as well as B2B services (logistics, manufacturing, consulting, and more). Tag your listings accurately so buyers posting matching requirements get connected to you automatically.
No. Listing your products or services on BigSpice is completely free. There are no subscription fees or listing charges. Optional paid advertising (homepage banner ads) is available if you want to boost your visibility.
When a buyer posts a requirement with a product or service tag, BigSpice automatically sends you an email notification if you have a listing with that same tag. This means you receive warm, pre-qualified leads without any extra effort. You can then message the buyer directly through the platform to discuss the deal.
After signing in, go to your Seller Dashboard. Click Add Listing and fill in the details: title, description, category, tags, price, unit, minimum order quantity, stock, and product images. You can edit or delete any listing at any time from your dashboard.
Buyer enquiries appear in the Messages section of your account. You’ll see a list of all conversations with buyers. Click any conversation to read and reply. Prompt responses improve your reputation on the platform.
Every seller gets a public store profile page showcasing their store name, logo, business type, categories, location, and all active listings. Buyers can browse your store and get in touch directly. Manage your store details from the Store Profile section.
Listings can be marked as Verified by the BigSpice admin team after review. Verified listings indicate that the product or service details have been confirmed and the seller is a legitimate business. This badge increases buyer confidence and can improve your listing’s visibility.

📣 Advertising

BigSpice offers banner ad slots on the homepage carousel, giving your business prominent visibility to all visitors. Sign up or log in as an Advertiser and go to your Advertiser Dashboard to submit a banner ad with your image and target URL.
Banner ads appear in the full-width homepage carousel. We recommend using high-resolution landscape images (ideally 1600×500 px or similar wide-format ratio) with clear branding and a compelling call-to-action. An admin will review your submission before it goes live.
Ad submissions are reviewed by the BigSpice admin team. Approval typically happens within 1–2 business days. You will be notified once your ad is approved and live on the platform.

⚙️ Account & Support

Yes. BigSpice supports businesses that both source and supply. You sign up with a primary role (buyer, seller, or advertiser), but the platform lets you engage on both sides. If you need to expand your role, update your profile settings or contact support.
Sign in and go to your Profile page. From there you can update your name, contact number, profile picture, business address, and other account details. Sellers can also update store information from the same area.
On the Sign in page, click Forgot password? and enter your registered email. You’ll receive a password reset link via email. Click the link, set a new password, and you’re good to go. If you don’t receive the email within a few minutes, check your spam folder or contact our support team.
If you encounter a fraudulent listing, spammy content, or suspicious behaviour, please send a message to our support team (see below) with the listing/user details. Our admin team reviews all reports and takes appropriate action to keep the platform trustworthy.
